Game Rules

Rock Paper Scissors is a simple hand game with the following rules:

  • Rock crushes Scissors (Rock wins)
  • Scissors cuts Paper (Scissors win)
  • Paper covers Rock (Paper wins)

If both players choose the same option, it's a tie and the round is repeated.

The first player to win 5 rounds wins the game!
